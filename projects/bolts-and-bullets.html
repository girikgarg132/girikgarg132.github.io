Combat Systems & Game Feel ("Juice")

In Bolts and Bullets, every shot, hit, and explosion was engineered with immense attention to kinetic feedback. To elevate the game beyond typical 2D shooters, I developed a suite of procedural game-feel modules:

  • Dynamic Screen Shake & Directional Impulse: Directional camera impulses that scale based on weapon caliber (shotguns trigger backwards kickback, high-caliber railguns induce slow-motion freeze frames).
  • Hit-Stop / Frame-Freeze (Micro-Stutter): A 3-frame freeze upon delivering critical headshots or dismantling heavy boss armor, creating high visceral impact.
  • Modular Weapon Customization: Dual-weapon loadouts featuring spread plasma, bounce flak, chain-lightning tasers, and homing swarm rockets.
  • Procedural Particle Explosions: Shrapnel sparks, lingering neon smoke trails, and physical robotic scraps that bounce off walls.

Enemy AI Architecture

Rogue robots are driven by behavioral tree state machines that execute coordinated pack hunting:

  • Scout Crawlers: Swarm the player in erratic zigzag paths to force movement.
  • Shield Bearers: Form defensive front lines that deflect kinetic projectiles, forcing the player to flank with dash maneuvers.
  • Heavy Goliaths: High-health walking fortresses firing predictive mortar arcs and sweeping lasers.
