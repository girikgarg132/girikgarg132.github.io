The Gameplay Core

Inspired by cult-classic 2D racers like Hill Climb Racing, Bhide Scooter Race centers around momentum conservation, center-of-mass balancing, and throttle control. Players pilot the legendary vintage scooter through unpredictable hill curves, dodging potholes, pulling off mid-air backflips, and collecting coins to fuel upgrades.

Physics Engine & Gameplay Programming

  • Dual Wheel-Joint 2D Suspension: Modeled realistic front-fork and rear spring suspension using configured Unity WheelJoint2D components, damping spring oscillations to prevent unnatural vehicle flipping.
  • Center-of-Mass & Air Torque Controls: Implemented responsive air-pitch torque allowing players to adjust their vehicle angle mid-jump to land cleanly on downhill slopes, rewarding skillful balance with turbo boost bursts.
  • Procedural Spline Terrain & Collision Smoothing: Leveraged 2D sprite shape controllers and edge colliders with optimized physics materials (friction & bounciness) to deliver silky smooth rolling without tire edge snagging.
  • Economy & Upgrade Shop Loop: Engineered an intuitive upgrade loop for Engine Power, Tire Grip, Suspension Damping, and Fuel Capacity with persistent cloud saves.

Key Takeaway

Achieving satisfying "game feel" in physics-based racers requires meticulous micro-tuning. A fraction of a millisecond difference in joint spring damping transforms a frustrating rollover into an exhilarating near-miss landing that keeps players hooked on "just one more run".
