The Musical Synchronization Core

In RE:Beat, the musical score isn't mere background decoration-it is the heartbeat of gameplay. The player's jumps, enemy laser pulses, and collapsing platforms occur in quantized sync with the song's BPM (beats per minute).

Missing a beat drops the player's momentum multiplier, while staying in the groove unlocks high-speed kinetic flow, allowing the player to bounce across impossible chasms.

Audio Engine & Gameplay Programming

  • DSP Audio Time Tracking: Used `AudioSettings.dspTime` to avoid frame-rate dependent audio desynchronization, guaranteeing sub-millisecond precision between audio playback and input judgment windows.
  • Visual Audio Spectrogram Integration: Visual environments pulse dynamically with frequency band spectrum analysis (FFT), causing level geometry to expand and glow on low-frequency bass drops.
  • Generative Jam Prototyping: Developed rapidly under strict game jam constraints with high-energy arcade scoring.
